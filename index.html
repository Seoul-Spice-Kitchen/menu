About Us

From Seoul to Your Table

In the heart of Uttara, Seoul Spice Kitchen brings you the authentic, homestyle flavors of Korea. Located at House 40, Road 12, every dish at our restaurant is prepared with love and care.

Our journey began with a simple goal - to share Korea's rich food culture with everyone. That's why every dish we make, from sizzling BBQ to Kimchi Jjigae, is crafted using only fresh ingredients.

At Seoul Spice Kitchen, we believe food isn't just about filling your stomach - it's an experience. Whether you're a longtime fan of Korean cuisine or trying it for the first time, we welcome you into our homely atmosphere.
